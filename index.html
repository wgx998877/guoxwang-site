Who am I?

Guoxin Wang (王国鑫) is a Principal Research Scientist in Azure Multimodal Intelligence Team of Microsoft Azure AI and work on natural language and document understanding projects in 2019. He joined in Big Data Mining and Knowledge Computing Group of Microsoft Research Asia (MSRA) in 2015.

His research interests broadly include: natural language processing, document understanding, named entity recognition and deep learning.
